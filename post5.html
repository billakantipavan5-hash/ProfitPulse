Affiliate Marketing Explained

Affiliate marketing is a method of earning money by promoting other companies’ products or services. You earn a commission for every successful sale. To start affiliate marketing, you need a platform such as a blog, YouTube channel, or social media account. You share affiliate links with your audience. When someone clicks your link and makes a purchase, you earn a commission. Popular affiliate programs include Amazon Associates and digital product platforms. Success in affiliate marketing depends on trust and quality content. Recommending useful products builds long-term income.
