Avoiding Online Scams

While earning online is real, scams are common. Beginners should be cautious when choosing opportunities. Avoid platforms that ask for high registration fees or promise unrealistic income. Genuine work focuses on skill and effort. Always research companies and read reviews before joining any online job. Staying informed helps protect your time and money.

🚨 Common Online Scams

1️⃣ “Pay ₹500–₹2000 Registration Fee”

“Work from home job” ani cheppi mundu money adugutaru.
👉 Genuine companies mundu money adagaru.

2️⃣ Too Good To Be True Offers

“Daily ₹5000 easy typing job”
“1 week lo ₹50,000 guarantee”
👉 Easy money ante mostly scam.

3️⃣ Fake Freelance Clients

Telegram lo message:
“I’ll pay $300, first send ₹1000 security fee”
🚫 Real client eppudu security money adagaru.

4️⃣ Fake Investment / Crypto Doubler

“Invest ₹2000 → get ₹8000 in 24 hours” 100% scam.

5️⃣ Phishing Links

Bank / Paytm / Amazon la fake websites create chesi OTP adugutaru.
👉 URL check chey
👉 HTTPS undha chudali
👉 OTP eppudu share cheyyakudadhu
