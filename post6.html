How Google AdSense Works

Google AdSense is an advertising program that allows website owners to earn money by displaying ads. It is beginner-friendly and widely used. After approval, ads are automatically shown on your website based on your content and visitors. You earn money when ads are viewed or clicked. To maximize earnings, focus on quality content and organic traffic. More visitors mean higher potential income. AdSense works best for blogs with consistent content and niche-focused audiences.
